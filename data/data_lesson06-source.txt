Lesson 06 classroom dataset: wage1

Source: Wooldridge wage1, 1976 US Current Population Survey (CPS).
Henry Farber supplied the source data to Jeffrey M. Wooldridge.
Documentation: https://justinmshea.github.io/wooldridge/reference/wage1.html
R package: https://CRAN.R-project.org/package=wooldridge
Package version used by the local source archive: 1.4-7.

The classroom CSV retains all 526 observations and selects four columns only:
wage: average hourly earnings, 1976 US dollars per hour.
educ: years of education.
exper: years of potential work experience; not actual measured tenure.
female: original dataset coding, 1=female, 0=male.
No observations were removed, simulated, or rounded.

This differs from the Japanese 2012 survey discussed in the course textbook.
Historical sample wage differences are associations, not identified causal
returns to education or direct measures of discrimination.
